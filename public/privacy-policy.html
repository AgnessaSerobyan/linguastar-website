Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for using LinguaStar. LinguaStar is provided by iSolutions OÜ (“we”, “our”, or “us”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the LinguaStar mobile app, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using LinguaStar, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate and improve our Service.

1.1. Information You Provide

  • Account data: such as your email address, password, and selected languages of study. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address that the provider shares with us.
  • Profile data: optional information like name, gender, or preferred language level.
  • Subscription data: information about your premium plan, purchase receipts, or renewal status (managed by the App Store).
  • Voice recordings: when you use pronunciation or conversation features, your speech samples are processed by AI models to recognize speech, analyze pronunciation and provide feedback.
    Voice recordings are stored for a month and are deleted permanently afterwards, together with any clips cut from them. The text of what you said stays in your conversation history. When you delete your account, we delete your recordings straight away. You can also ask us to delete them at any time (see section 6).
    We do not sell, share, or reuse your voice recordings for marketing or external purposes.
  • Learning content: text you enter, conversation messages, practice sentences, and related learning content. We process this content to provide AI responses, translations, pronunciation feedback, and spoken audio. Text used for speech generation may include personal information that you include in a message or that appears in a tutor's reply.

1.2. Automatically Collected Information

When you use LinguaStar, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, region, time zone).
  • App usage data (features used, screens viewed, session length, purchase steps, crash logs).
  • Analytics data: we record usage events in our own analytics system and with Google Analytics for Firebase. These events are linked to your app installation and, if you have an account, to your account.
  • Attribution data: if you installed LinguaStar after tapping an Apple Search Ads ad, Apple tells us which campaign led to the installation.

This information helps us improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and personalize the language-learning experience
  • Analyze pronunciation and track learning progress
  • Improve AI accuracy and app performance
  • Manage subscriptions and account access
  • Understand which features and campaigns bring people to LinguaStar
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We never use your personal or voice data to train external AI models or for advertising targeting.

3. Data Storage and Retention

We and our service providers store and process information in the locations where our systems and services operate. Depending on the service, this may include countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. In particular, Google Cloud Gemini speech generation uses a global endpoint and is not restricted to processing within the European Union.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service or meet legal requirements. Retention by a service provider can differ from retention in LinguaStar. Google may retain request data for limited periods for security, abuse monitoring, and service operation under its applicable terms and settings. We do not represent Gemini processing as having zero data retention.

Google Cloud's Data Processing Addendum describes its data-protection obligations and applicable international-transfer safeguards. You can contact us at support@linguastar.ai for information about how we handle your data or to exercise the rights described in section 6.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal or voice data. We may share limited data only in the following cases:

  • Service providers: trusted partners who help us operate the Service, including:
    • hosting, databases and file storage (Heroku, Redis Cloud, Google Cloud Storage);
    • AI conversation, speech recognition and speech generation (OpenAI, Google Cloud Gemini);
    • pronunciation assessment (SpeechSuper), which receives your voice recordings;
    • analytics and error reporting (Google Firebase, Sentry);
    • subscriptions and payments (Apple, RevenueCat);
    • sign-in (Apple, Google) and email delivery (MXroute).
  • Legal compliance: when required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

Google Cloud: we may use Google Cloud Gemini to turn tutor messages and practice text into spoken audio. For this purpose, we send the text selected for speech generation together with voice and speaking-style instructions. Google processes that content to provide the speech service under its applicable Google Cloud terms and Data Processing Addendum.

We share with each provider only the information it needs to provide its service to us, and we use providers that commit to protecting personal data under their terms.

5. AI and Speech Processing

LinguaStar uses cloud AI services to provide conversations, translations, pronunciation feedback, and spoken examples. The information processed depends on the feature you use.

For Google Cloud Gemini speech generation, the input is text, such as a tutor's reply or a practice sentence. The output is generated speech. This speech-generation request does not include your microphone recording, account password, payment details, or a separately attached email address or account identifier. However, the text itself may contain personal information, including information you shared during a conversation.

This differs from pronunciation assessment and speech recognition, which may process your voice recordings as described elsewhere in this policy. Generated audio may be stored by LinguaStar so that it can be played again.

Google's applicable Cloud terms prohibit using Customer Data to train or fine-tune AI models without our prior permission or instruction. This restriction is separate from Google's retention of data for security and service operation. Please avoid including sensitive information in practice text or conversations when it is not needed for language learning.

We do not use your recordings for marketing, profiling, or public sharing.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or limit certain types of processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: support@linguastar.ai

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and voice data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. While no system is completely secure, we continuously improve our practices to keep your data safe.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in the app and on our website. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: 📧 support@linguastar.ai
